# Delivery delays — ART Style 2 design plan

Exact user-supplied copy is preserved in `source-content.json`. This is a proposed visual plan, not a rendered proof or publication approval.

## Direction

An early-warning story: catch the small slip, make time to respond, then let the customer hear from your team. Premium isolated 3D logistics objects; no mascot, warehouse photographs, people or product screens. Use Style 2's dark/aqua/light rhythm, static readable text and a single calm object moment per slide.

Style 2 typography: Poppins capitals on the cover, Preahvihear headlines and Poppins supporting text. This differs intentionally from Style 3's all-Preahvihear rule. No copy rewritten. Cover uses the supplied hook and pride-led question; no added slogan or claim.

## Production order

1. Approve this object/layout plan.
2. Generate matching cut-out objects through the chosen image route; inspect each.
3. Build cover and slide 4 first, checking the exact text, fonts and object boundaries.
4. Share the two proofs before building/rendering all nine.
5. Complete the remaining slides and verify actual exports.

## Image route

The guide defaults to free generation in Google AI Studio/Gemini. `images/PROMPTS.md` provides the complete prompts and instructions. An in-chat generation route can be used if explicitly selected; do not assume a free allowance or a paid budget.

## Approval requirement

The selected [Style 2 guide](../../source-documents/ART-carousel-design-style2.md), workflow step 2, says: “Plan objects and moments, get an OK” and “Wait for approval.” This plan is prepared for that review. It does not require re-approving the supplied copy; any capability or publishing sign-off remains separate.

## Revised first two proofs after user feedback

The user rejected the initial overall design. Cover: replace the cropped van with a 3D parcel/bell and a graphic split showing the alert reaching the team first. Transit: retain the truck as a secondary context object, make a stopped shipment signal the primary explanatory visual, and reveal a warning at the stopping point. Simplify decoration, strengthen hierarchy, and keep the Style 2 palette and fonts. These revisions await two-proof review before the rest is completed.

## Selected direction: B — Race to know

The user chose B after comparing three visual concepts. Develop the aqua early-alert shortcut versus the longer ivory customer route throughout the story. The first two implementations are the selected cover and a new stalled-truck/shortcut scene for slide 4. Animate only the alert along the short route; keep the route sculpture, truck and typography stable. Await review of these implementations before completing the remaining seven slides.
