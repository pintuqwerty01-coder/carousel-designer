# Style 2 — three visual directions

These are static cover concept previews, not completed carousels. The same supplied copy and Style 2 palette/fonts are used for all three so the visual idea is the deciding variable. Original approved/rejected proof files remain unchanged.

| Option | Core idea | Motion after selection | In-transit extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — Domino effect | Catch the first slipping parcel before the chain tips | A warning appears at the caught first tile; the remainder stays stable | A stalled tile creates a gap; the early alert isolates it |
| B — Race to know | A shorter alert route reaches the team first | An alert travels the aqua shortcut while the longer customer route waits | A shipment stalls on the main route; its alert takes the shortcut |
| C — Blind spot | Shine a light on a hidden delay | A restrained light reveals the warning inside the crate | A stalled shipment is revealed at the checkpoint before the customer asks |

Option letters/titles are review labels, not marketing-script additions. No new claims, timestamps, deadlines or metric numbers are introduced. 3D shapes are conceptual props, not actual customers or product screens. No animation is being delivered at this selection step.

Deliverables: `compare.png`, three `option-*.png` covers and `concept-options.pdf` (three pages, A/B/C). The chosen idea will be developed into a cover plus one task proof before the remaining slides.
