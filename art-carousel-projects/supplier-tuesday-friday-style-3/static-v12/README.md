# Supplier follow-through — six-slide static design v12

Current user-authorized design script: six slides, ending with a supplier-workflow CTA. The governance addition is confined to slide 5. This is a static review, with no motion exports.

## Scene and message alignment

| Slide | Message | Scene |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ART tracks the commitment and prepares the next move | Original white-limb mascot points at the Tuesday/Friday delivery record. |
| 2 | ART keeps requesting updates and sends timely reminders | Original white-limb phone scene, with editable dispatch/arrival questions and reminder status. No new delivery or supplier reply is invented. |
| 3 | Stock coverage, shortfall and orders at risk | Original white-limb mascot inspects receiving documents beside stock cartons. |
| 4 | Find a suitable approved alternate | New localized prop/gesture adaptation: same established mascot compares physical quotation cards in the existing warehouse setting. No invented prices or supplier promises. |
| 5 | Account for original supply; operate within purchasing rules; refer exceptions | Original folder handover to a human, plus two distinct rule boxes. No repetition of the supporting sentence in the boxes. |
| 6 | Topic-specific CTA | Original white-limb mascot and correspondence folders, blue/black logo and invitation to discuss supplier workflow. |

Five original scene files are referenced directly from static-v9, unchanged. Slide 4 keeps the white-limb mascot design reference. Arms, hands and legs remain white; head/torso aqua; shoes black. No top ART watermark. Preahvihear throughout; regular only, with no synthetic bold. All editable text, logo and boxes remain separate from scene images.

`content.json` is canonical for this six-slide design. `build.py` generates editable compositions. `render.py` verifies actual fonts, canonical text, rule boxes, CTA, safe margins and overflow; outputs native 1080×1350 PNGs, quality-98 JPEGs, an ordered PDF retaining vector text, an overview and ZIP. Historical eight-slide versions are retained separately.
