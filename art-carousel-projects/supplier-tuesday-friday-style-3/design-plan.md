# Scene-specific visual story — v3

| Slide | Message | Setting / props | Mascot action and expression |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Missed delivery, ART already working | Receiving bay; Tuesday/Friday clipboard | Points at the overdue record; alert and determined |
| 2 | Downstream work waits | Paused packing conveyor, empty parts tray | Open hand toward missing item; concerned |
| 3 | Detect the missed date | Inspection bench, expected and receiving records | Magnifier over the records; focused |
| 4 | Request a revised date | Supplier coordination desk, outgoing message on phone | Presses send; helpful and active |
| 5 | Track a response | Angled office desk, tablet and stylus | Updates the response record; concentrating |
| 6 | Escalate unanswered delay | Ink-toned operations desk, unresolved folder and warning flag | Flags the issue; concerned urgency |
| 7 | Keep the next decision human | Ink-toned review desk and colleague's hand | Hands over context dossier; calm and attentive |
| 8 | Let ART do the chasing | Ink-toned desk, organized correspondence folders | Confident and relaxed, grounded beside folders |

Upper copy uses the same Preahvihear hierarchy and fitted card language. Photo-overlay shading fades into each scene. The last three scenes share the dark closing palette. Slide 5 is the response-received branch; slide 6 is the no-answer branch. Neither promises an arrival or automatically chooses another supplier.
