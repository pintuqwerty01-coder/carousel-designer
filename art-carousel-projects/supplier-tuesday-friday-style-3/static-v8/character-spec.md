# ART character consistency specification

Canonical identity: `../slides/assets/mascot-point.png`. Use this same model reference for every scene, never the preceding generated scene as the only identity reference.

Keep one physical model across all slides:

- Chunky dimensional block construction; rectangular stepped head and inset white face. Same head depth, stepped side pieces and edge bevels.
- Head width about 1.55 × torso width. Head height about 1.1 × torso height. Full white leg length about 0.8 × torso height. These are working visual targets, assessed with allowance for perspective and occlusion.
- Aqua/cyan body from the canonical reference, white face/gloves/lower legs, black block shoes and black antenna stalk. Lighting may shade the surfaces but must not change the material to blue, green or a different finish.
- One centered antenna with a single aqua cube cap. One white rectangular chest panel with two aqua square openings. Preserve their scale and construction.
- Same segmented white-and-aqua arm thickness, block gloves and finger construction. Same leg thickness and shoe volume.
- Same underlying face grid and feature weight. Two coherent eyes and one simple mouth. Expressions vary with the message; face anatomy and grid do not.
- Comparable overall character scale across the carousel, approximately half the frame height including antenna, with feet grounded on the table near the same lower scene band. Perspective and purposeful leaning can affect projected size, but must not squash the body or shorten limbs.

Pose and contact checks: pointing to clipboard; palm toward empty tray; leaning at hips/knees to inspect records; touching phone; stylus contacting tablet; flag gripped in hand; folder supported during human handoff; arm resting on correspondence stack. Keep all original scene props and blank surfaces for editable text overlays.
