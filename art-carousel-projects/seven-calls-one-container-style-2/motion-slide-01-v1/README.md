# Seven calls — slide 1 motion proof

20 seconds, 1080 × 1350, H.264, 24 fps. The first 13 seconds follow the six-beat storyboard: port and ART enter, the seven named calls accumulate, ART reacts, and the cover heading and supporting sentence appear. The completed cover holds from roughly 13 seconds to the end so the text can be read.

The copy is verbatim from the user's seven-slide PDF: “7 CALLS. STILL CHASING ONE CONTAINER.” and “Every update creates another check, another change, another follow-up—for you.” The same seven call labels from the motion storyboard are Booking, Packing List, Pickup, Delay Update, Truck Timing, Loading Window and Customs Check. The mascot keeps its approved cubic aqua body, white arms and legs, black shoes, white face and torso mark. Its pose changes to act out the supplied storyline.

The source PDF is a single flattened image per page, so the port photo and mascot poses were prepared as separate layers for animation. `index.html` holds editable type, call cards, icons, connections and timing. `render.py` captures the layout in Chromium and encodes the MP4 with ffmpeg. `delivery/motion-keyframes-v1.jpg` shows the seven reviewed points in the video. The final composition is held without further movement from about 13.5 to 20 seconds.

Source references: user's `ART_Container_Carousel_7_Slides.pdf`, supplied six-panel slide 1 motion storyboard, and the repository's ART mascot assets. This is a motion proof for slide 1, not an animation of slides 2–7.
