# Container cover — Style 1 motion-beat revision

This is the existing “7 CALLS. STILL CHASING ONE CONTAINER.” cover, revised as a single 10-second motion slide. Its headline is present and stationary from frame one. The supporting sentence appears near the end and the completed composition holds for roughly three seconds. The seven call labels, port setting and mascot design are retained.

The motion uses the applicable Style 1 principles: the mascot has separate entrance, tablet-check and overwhelmed poses; its position snaps to a 4 px grid and pose changes occur on 8 fps beats; the call cards and connection lines ease smoothly; links render behind the mascot; and no card covers its face or body. The 3D mascot appearance is retained because that was the approved look for this container cover. The older Style 1 flat pixel mascot was not substituted.

`index.html` contains editable type, cards and timing; `render.py` exports the 1080 × 1350, 30 fps, H.264 MP4. The eight keyframes in `delivery/motion-keyframes-v4.jpg` show the entrance, seven-call buildup, reaction and closing hold. The source background is a clean motion plate derived from the user's flattened carousel PDF, as recorded in the first motion proof.
