## Main distinction from recent rejected/simplified proofs

Style 2 has a deliberately dramatic editorial composition, not simply a minimal centred diagram on a gradient. The cutouts, off-centre text, oversized cropped words/numbers and light effects carry its identity. Keep negative space, but make the object and text arrangement deliberate. Calm motion does not require removing the characteristic light effects or oversized graphic elements.
